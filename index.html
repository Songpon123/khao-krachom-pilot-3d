Plot facts

Camera trap

LiDAR values inside the 25 m and 50 m buffers around the camera, from camera_buffer_metrics.csv (Aug 2026 flights). These are the numbers one camera site contributes to the mammal model.

Herp survey points

Proposed sites (teal) on low ground next to drainage lines inferred from the CSF DTM: lowest 30% of the plot, lower than the ground within 15 m, ≤10 m from a channel, ≥30 m apart. Check each one in the field.
